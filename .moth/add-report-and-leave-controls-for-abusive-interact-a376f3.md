The app pairs strangers but has no report/block flow. Leaving alone does not provide a way to flag abuse or avoid immediately being paired with the same person.

Acceptance criteria:

- Provide a clear report-and-leave action for both roles.
- Prevent immediate rematching with a reported participant within a defined session policy.
- Define how reports are reviewed and what minimal data is collected, with explicit retention and disclosure.
- Do not silently start storing all transcripts or drafts.
- Test normal leaving, malicious reports, repeat pairing and accessible mobile controls.
